# World Happiness Report — Country Clustering
### (Unsupervised learning)
## Objective
Group countries into clusters based on the socio-economic factors that 
contribute to happiness (GDP per capita, social support, life expectancy, 
freedom, generosity, trust in government), in order to identify distinct 
"happiness profiles" across the world.

## Data Source
World Happiness Report, years 2015-2019.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/unsdsn/world-happiness

## Task Type
Unsupervised Learning — Clustering
(No target/label column; the goal is to discover natural groupings, 
not to predict a known outcome.)

## Features Used for Clustering
- GDP per capita
- Social support (Family)
- Healthy life expectancy
- Freedom to make life choices
- Perceptions of corruption (Trust)
- Generosity

## Business/Analytical Question
Which countries share similar happiness drivers, and do these clusters 
align with geographic regions — or reveal unexpected groupings?
